## Math check: restriction energy identity

Reconstructed the HD restriction identity independently, following the physical
energy accounting discussed in Schmidt et al. (2014). Assume equal-volume child
cells, strictly positive density and conservative averaging of mass and momentum.

Define

$$
\rho_c=\frac1N\sum_n\rho_n,\quad
\boldsymbol m_c=\frac1N\sum_n\rho_n\boldsymbol u_n,\quad
\boldsymbol u_c=\frac{\boldsymbol m_c}{\rho_c}.
$$

Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1{2N}\sum_n\rho_n|\boldsymbol u_n-\boldsymbol u_c|^2
&=\frac1{2N}\sum_n\rho_n|\boldsymbol u_n|^2
-\boldsymbol u_c\cdot\frac1N\sum_n\rho_n\boldsymbol u_n
+\frac12|\boldsymbol u_c|^2\frac1N\sum_n\rho_n\\
&=\frac1{2N}\sum_n\rho_n|\boldsymbol u_n|^2
-\boldsymbol u_c\cdot\boldsymbol m_c
+\frac12\rho_c|\boldsymbol u_c|^2\\
&=\frac1{2N}\sum_n\rho_n|\boldsymbol u_n|^2
-\frac12\rho_c|\boldsymbol u_c|^2=\delta K.
\end{aligned}
$$

It is nonnegative as a density-weighted variance. With $U_n=\rho_n k_n$
denoting the fine SGS energy density, set

$$
U_c=\frac1N\sum_n U_n+\delta K.
$$

Substitution gives

$$
\frac12\rho_c|\boldsymbol u_c|^2+U_c
=\frac1N\sum_n\left(\frac12\rho_n|\boldsymbol u_n|^2+U_n\right).
$$

All terms have energy-density dimensions $[M L^{-1}T^{-2}]$.
Uniform velocity gives $\delta K=0$, including nonuniform density.
Common velocity boosts leave the variance unchanged. Unequal child volumes require
volume-weighted averages rather than $1/N$.

In-session arithmetic checks used 1,000 randomly generated eight-cell states with
positive densities, three velocity components and nonnegative SGS energy. The
variance identity, nonnegativity and energy equality all passed with absolute
tolerance $10^{-12}$ in arbitrary test units. The largest measured energy residual
was $2.842170943040401\times10^{-14}$.

A two-cell illustration used $\rho_1=1$, $\rho_2=3$, $u_1=0$, $u_2=2$ in consistent
arbitrary units. It gives $\rho_c=2$, $u_c=3/2$, fine mean kinetic density $3$,
coarse resolved kinetic density $9/4$, and $\delta K=3/4$. Assigning that difference
to SGS energy restores the represented kinetic sum.

**Checks out within these assumptions.** This verifies a restriction identity,
not a solver, a refinement reconstruction or a physical turbulence closure.
The variance also includes coherent gradients and shocks; the turbulence
interpretation requires additional modelling. Refinement needs positivity and
reconstruction decisions not supplied by the identity.
